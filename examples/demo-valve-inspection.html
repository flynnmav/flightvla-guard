安全边界 Safety boundary: 本平台中 VLA / VLM / LLM 只允许输出 ActionBlock(短时 6-DoF 动作块: frame / horizon / dt / delta_position / delta_orientation / stop_probability / confidence)。 电机 PWM、电机转速、单旋翼推力、PX4 actuator command、绕过安全层的 MAVLink 指令在 schema 层即无法表达; SafetyGuard 是 offboard setpoint 流的唯一写入者。PX4 始终拥有 EKF 与位置/速度/姿态/角速度闭环; offboard 心跳中断时 PX4 退出 offboard 并执行其 failsafe —— 这就是本运行时的执行边界。
EN: Agents emit ActionBlocks only. Motor PWM / rotor speed / per-rotor thrust / PX4 actuator commands / raw MAVLink are unrepresentable at the schema level. The guard is the sole writer of the offboard stream; PX4 keeps owning the EKF and every closed loop, and its own failsafe is the final execution boundary.
